The 7VN perspective

People remember how a place makes them feel.

It might be the restroom they visit, the table where they sit, or the break room where their day begins. The essentials rarely take center stage. But when they're missing, everyone notices.

We believe attention to everyday comfort is one of the quietest ways a business can show it cares.

Our approach

Part of 7VN Designs LLC, our new venture brings the independent, brand-first approach behind 7VN Grounds to the everyday supplies that help workplaces and guest spaces feel cared for.
